Morgan’s View

A house on the headland, where the lawn runs out and the Indian Ocean starts. Whole house, nine beds, and a deck built for doing nothing.

The welcome

We built this house for the view, and then found ourselves living on the deck.

Morgan’s View sits high on the point above the main beach, with the sea on one side and grazing land on the other. Everything is on one level, open-plan and full of light: a long kitchen island where people gather, a lounge that opens to the wraparound deck, and bedrooms that all catch the morning.

It is a quiet house in a quiet village. Come for early swims, long lunches, walks to the cliffs, and the sound of the surf at night. It is not the house for a loud party — and we say that kindly, so nobody is disappointed.

The village

Morgan Bay does very little, very well.

A handful of streets, a long stretch of sand, a lagoon at one end and the cliffs at the other. There is a hotel with a restaurant and a pub, a small shop for the essentials, and not much else to interrupt you.

The beach
A wide, long stretch of sand a short walk down from the house, with rock pools at low tide.
The cliffs
The walk to the Morgan Bay cliffs and back is the thing everyone remembers. Go late afternoon.
The lagoon
Flat water at the far end of the beach for paddling, kayaks and small children.
Eating out
The hotel deck, Salty or Bayview restaurant are options. Book ahead in season.
Further afield
Kei Mouth and the ferry across to the Wild Coast make an easy day trip.
Good to know

A quiet house, honestly described.

Morgan’s View sleeps nine, with a maximum of six adults. It is a noise-sensitive house with neighbours close by, so it suits families and small groups rather than celebrations. If you are looking for somewhere to be loud, we would rather tell you now than after you arrive.
